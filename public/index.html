Rules

1. Hold review requests during an open complaint
Tags the customer dp-hold-review in Shopify. In Judge.me, add that tag to the customer blacklist (Judge.me skips tagged customers). The tag comes off when the ticket closes, or after days at most, so requests are delayed, never cancelled.
2. Pause promotional emails during an open complaint
Sets dp_open_complaint on the customer's Klaviyo profile (true while the complaint is open). In your promotional flows, add a profile filter: dp_open_complaint is not true.
3. Flag products with sizing or quality complaints
complaints about one product in 30 days tags it dp-sizing-watch or dp-quality-watch in Shopify, with Inventory Planner's reorder recommendation shown in the log so you can check before reordering.
4. Put your best customers first
Open tickets from customers who have spent at least get the Gorgias tag decisionporter-vip. In Gorgias, create a view or rule that puts this tag at the top.

Gorgias and the Judge.me spam check use simple, transparent keyword/length heuristics — real starting points, not trained models. Klaviyo and Inventory Planner decisions use each platform's own real computed numbers (churn probability, days to stockout), and Judge.me's founder-reply and photo flags use real review fields (rating, published photos) — DecisionPorter mostly applies thresholds and routes or surfaces the result.
